# StockMarketLoop Discord Verification Privacy Notice

**Last updated: September 28, 2026**

This notice applies to the StockMarketLoop Discord verification page used to protect servers from automated accounts, duplicate accounts and ban evasion. It supplements the StockMarketLoop Privacy Policy and is shown before a member submits the verification check.

## What is collected

The verification page receives the answers to the server-rules quiz and the following security signals:

- Discord user ID, server ID, account-creation age and verification result;
- a keyed fingerprint of the connecting IP address and, where available, its network prefix;
- an approximate country code supplied by the hosting edge, when available;
- user-agent, browser/device category, time zone, language, screen-size class and automation signals; and
- a random browser marker supplied by the member's browser to identify repeated verification attempts from the same browser.

Raw IP addresses are not written to the verification database. The fingerprints are keyed so they cannot be used as a general-purpose lookup or location service. No precise location is inferred or shared with members.

## Purpose and decisions

Signals are used only for server security, duplicate-account review, ban-evasion investigation and resolving verification appeals. A signal can place a verification in moderator review; it is not proof of wrongdoing and does not automatically identify a person outside the server. Moderators may approve or deny a held verification after review.

The system does not collect Discord passwords or tokens, read direct messages, access private channels, access brokerage or payment credentials, or sell verification data. It does not attempt to locate or track a member outside the server.

## Sharing, access and retention

The service host and authorized StockMarketLoop administrators may process the data to operate and secure verification. Verification data is not shown to other Discord members. Records are retained for up to 180 days for security, fraud review, appeals and legal obligations, then deleted by the cleanup job unless a shorter deletion is requested and legally permitted.

## Member choices and requests

Verification is required only for servers that have enabled this gate. A member may decline, but will not receive the server's verified posting access. Members may request access, correction or deletion of their verification data, or appeal a held/denied result, by contacting **vaughnmcnair0@gmail.com** and including their Discord user ID and server ID. Do not send passwords, tokens or payment information.

We may update this notice when the verification flow changes. The page will display the current notice before data is submitted.
