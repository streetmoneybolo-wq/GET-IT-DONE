# StockMarketLoop Discord Role Sync Privacy Notice

**Last updated: August 23, 2026**

This Privacy Notice explains how StockMarketLoop uses information when you choose to connect your Discord account to your StockMarketLoop account to receive Making Easy Money group access based on your Discord role. It supplements the main StockMarketLoop Privacy Policy.

## 1. What this integration does

The Discord Role Sync feature lets a member voluntarily link a Discord account to a StockMarketLoop account. After a successful link, the feature checks whether that Discord account holds one of the specifically mapped Making Easy Money Discord roles and applies the corresponding website group role. It does not make trading decisions, place trades, or access a member's financial accounts.

## 2. Information we collect

When you use the feature, we process only the information needed to link the accounts and manage access:

- Your StockMarketLoop account ID;
- Your Discord user ID and current Discord username or display tag;
- The Discord server ID and the IDs of your Discord roles used for the access decision;
- The resulting StockMarketLoop group-access role, connection time, and access-change audit details; and
- A one-time account-linking code. The website stores only a SHA-256 hash of that code. The code expires after 10 minutes and is single-use.

## 3. Information we do not collect through Role Sync

The Role Sync feature does **not** read or store your Discord password, Discord authentication token, direct-message content, private-channel messages, payment-card information, brokerage credentials, or Discord message history. It does not post messages or change roles in your Discord account.

## 4. How we use the information

We use the information above solely to:

- confirm that a Discord account is linked to the correct StockMarketLoop account;
- map eligible Discord roles to Making Easy Money website group access;
- refresh that access when you use the available refresh command;
- prevent duplicate or unauthorized account links; and
- investigate or resolve access, fraud, abuse, or security issues.

We do not sell Role Sync personal information. We do not use Role Sync information for targeted advertising.

## 5. Sharing and service providers

Discord receives information required to operate its own platform under Discord's privacy policy. StockMarketLoop's bot sends the minimum information required to the StockMarketLoop website to complete the link and access check. Authorized StockMarketLoop administrators and service providers that host or secure the website may process this information only to operate, secure, and support the feature.

## 6. Your choices and controls

Linking Discord is optional. If you do not link your account, the role-sync feature will not grant Discord-based website access.

You may disconnect your Discord account at any time at:

`https://stockmarketloop.com/connect-discord/`

Disconnecting removes the account link and revokes only group access that this feature created. It does **not** alter membership or access that was granted manually or through a separate paid membership process. Security and access audit records may be retained as necessary to protect the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

## 7. Security

We use access controls, a short-lived hashed link code, server-to-server authentication, and role IDs rather than mutable role names to reduce the risk of unauthorized access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and you should never share a one-time linking code with anyone else.

## 8. Data retention

We retain connection and role-grant records while your Discord account remains linked and for as long as needed to operate the feature. One-time linking codes expire after 10 minutes and are deleted after use. We may retain limited access audit records for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, and legal compliance.
